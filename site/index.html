Tonearm GitHub

A mini player for Spotify in the Mac menu bar

Click the song in the menu bar to see the cover, title and artist. Play, pause, skip, seek and change the volume without switching to the Spotify app.

Download for Mac

Free and open source. macOS 13 or later, Apple Silicon and Intel.

Or with Homebrew

brew install --cask abulkosim/tap/tonearm

What it does

  • The song in the menu bar

    The title, the title and artist, or just an icon. You set how long it gets.

  • Cover and colors

    The player shows the cover on a background taken from its colors. A compact layout keeps it small.

  • All the controls

    Play, pause, previous, next, shuffle and repeat. Drag the bars to seek or to change Spotify's volume.

  • Keyboard

    Space plays or pauses, the arrow keys seek and change the volume. ⌃⌥P ⌃⌥N ⌃⌥B work in any app if you turn them on.

  • No account

    Tonearm talks to the Spotify app on your Mac through AppleScript. No login, no Web API, no analytics.

  • Updates itself

    It checks once a day and installs new versions. The Spotify permission carries over.

First launch

  1. 1

    Open Tonearm. Tonearm is not notarized by Apple, so macOS blocks it the first time. Close the message.

  2. 2

    Open System Settings, then Privacy & Security, and click Open Anyway. On macOS 13 and 14 you can right-click Tonearm in Applications and choose Open instead. Homebrew installs skip this step.

  3. 3

    Click OK when macOS asks to let Tonearm control Spotify. Then look for Tonearm in the menu bar, next to the clock. It has no Dock icon. If it doesn't show up, see the questions.
