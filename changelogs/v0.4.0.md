# v0.4.0

2026-11-18 · [Download](https://github.com/Abulkosim/tonearm/releases/tag/v0.4.0)

- New: Liner Notes. Tonearm now keeps a record of what you play, and shows your top artists, songs and albums, minutes per day and when you listen, for any week, month, year or all time. Open it with the chart button next to the gear, or ⌘L.
- Import Spotify's data download to see years of listening at once. Spotify > Account > Privacy > Download your data.
- Save any period as a picture to share.
- Your history stays on your Mac. Settings > History turns recording off, exports everything as CSV or JSON, or deletes it. You can also send your plays to ListenBrainz; that is off unless you turn it on.
- Settings scroll on short screens instead of being cut off.
- Long song names fade out instead of stopping mid-word. The menu bar title ends at a whole word and keeps the artist.
- Arabic and other right-to-left titles line up correctly, and songs of unknown length show the time played.
