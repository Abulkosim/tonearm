# v0.1.0

2026-11-10 · [Download](https://github.com/Abulkosim/tonearm/releases/tag/v0.1.0)

- First release. Tonearm is a menu bar player for the Spotify desktop app on macOS 13 and later.
- The menu bar shows the current song title, or just an icon if you prefer.
- Click it to open a popover with the cover, title and artist.
- Play, pause, previous and next buttons.
- Drag the progress bar to seek. Drag the volume bar to change Spotify's volume.
- The popover background takes its colors from the cover.
- When Spotify is closed, the popover says so and has a button to open it.
- Option to launch Tonearm at login.
- Tonearm talks to Spotify through AppleScript only. No account, login or API keys. macOS asks once for permission to control Spotify.
