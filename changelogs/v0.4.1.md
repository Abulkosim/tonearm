# v0.4.1

2026-11-22 · [Download](https://github.com/Abulkosim/tonearm/releases/tag/v0.4.1)

- Fixes the menu bar going blank now and then when Spotify starts playing after a quiet spell.
- Liner Notes opens fast after a restart, even with years of imported history. A history imported with 0.4.0 is tidied up once at the first launch.
- VoiceOver reads the position, the volume, what is playing in the menu bar, and every value in the Liner Notes charts.
- Skipping through songs posts one notification, for the song you stay on.
- Space presses the focused button when you move around with the keyboard.
- Liner Notes follows your Mac to a new time zone.
- If Tonearm is stopped in the middle of an import, the unpacked copy of your Spotify download is cleaned up. Delete all history also removes the share card and any plays waiting to go to ListenBrainz.
