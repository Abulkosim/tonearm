# v0.2.0

2026-11-10 · [Download](https://github.com/Abulkosim/tonearm/releases/tag/v0.2.0)

- The window now fits its content. Before, the settings panel floated inside a window as tall as the player.
- Shuffle and repeat buttons.
- Hover the cover to copy the song link or open the song in Spotify. Clicking the title also opens Spotify.
- Keyboard shortcuts while the window is open: Space, ← →, ⌘← ⌘→, ↑ ↓, S, R, ⌘, and ⌘Q.
- Optional global shortcuts that work anywhere: ⌃⌥P play or pause, ⌃⌥N next, ⌃⌥B previous.
- Compact layout: a small cover next to the title in a short window.
- Optional notification when the song changes.
- Darker background colors taken from the cover, and a cleaner settings panel.
- Shows "Waiting for permission" while the macOS permission dialog is open.
- Fixed Spotify reopening right after you quit it.
- Fixed the volume bar and play button jumping back after a click.
- Podcasts, local files and ads no longer show as "Nothing playing".
- Fixed seek and volume stopping after Spotify quit during a change.
- Covers load again after a failed download, and refresh after the Mac wakes from sleep.
